# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive community. Everyone
participating in this project is expected to act with empathy, patience, and
respect regardless of experience, identity, background, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent while discussing ideas.
- Give specific, actionable feedback about the work, not the person.
- Accept corrections gracefully and help newcomers find relevant context.
- Protect private information and follow responsible security disclosure.
- Respect maintainer decisions about scope while remaining free to disagree
  constructively.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized content, deliberate
misgendering, doxxing, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's
private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock conversations, reject
contributions, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. Enforcement decisions will consider context, severity, prior
behavior, and community safety.

Report an incident privately through the repository's
[private reporting form](https://github.com/suguspnk/openmergelens/security/advisories/new)
and begin the report title with `Code of conduct:`. Do not include sensitive
details in a public issue. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical, and retaliation against reporters is prohibited.
